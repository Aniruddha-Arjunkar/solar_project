Yes, **we can create a single table**, especially for the current stage 
of your Solar Services CRM. It will make the backend simpler to understand 
and implement.

But there is an important distinction: **one table for everything** 
can be simple initially, while becoming messy once the application grows.

For your project, I would actually recommend a **single main `leads` table 
first**, rather than immediately creating separate tables for Inquiry, 
Follow-up, Visit, Schedule, Service, Future Client, and Quotation.

### Why a single table can work well here

Your frontend already follows a common structure:

```text

   ↓

   ↓
Visit
   ↓
```
